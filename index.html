PRACTICE SYSTEM

Learn by building

Professional learning checklist

  • Set up a legal home lab and practice only on systems you own or are authorized to test.
  • Keep a learning journal with commands, concepts, mistakes and solutions.
  • Complete small projects and write clear documentation for each one.
  • Learn to read logs and explain what happened—not just which tool produced an output.
  • Build a portfolio with defensive projects, write-ups and measurable outcomes.
  • Prepare for interviews by explaining fundamentals in simple language.

Golden rules

① Authorization first
② Understand before automating
③ Practice in safe labs
④ Document everything
⑤ Keep learning
